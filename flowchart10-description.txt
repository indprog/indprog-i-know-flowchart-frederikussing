Process: Checking if the beef is ready

Start by measuring the beef temperature.

If the beef temperature is greater than or equal to 50 degrees Celsius, output "Beef is ready", take the beef off the stove, and end the process.

If the beef temperature is below 50 degrees Celsius, output "Beef not done", leave the beef on the stove, and measure the beef temperature again.

Repeat this process until the beef temperature is greater than or equal to 50 degrees Celsius.
